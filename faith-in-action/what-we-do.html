Ministry Pillars

What We Do

Practical solutions that create lasting transformation — from fields in Africa to neighborhoods in Texas.

We work across interconnected areas that open pathways out of poverty, protect the vulnerable, and restore dignity. Sustainable agriculture is at the center of long-term change abroad; local outreach and advocacy against domestic violence are core to our work in Texas.

01
Clean Water

Water changes everything.

When a community gains safe, reliable water, health improves, children can stay in school, and women and girls are freed from hours of daily fetching. Clean water is often the first step toward lasting transformation.

We fund and partner on wells, boreholes, storage systems, filtration, and community training so water access is sustainable — not a one-time gift.

  • Wells and boreholes in underserved villages
  • Filtration and safe storage solutions
  • Local training for maintenance and ownership
Give Water
02
Sustainable Agriculture

The core of lasting change.

Many families have land but lack quality seeds, tools, irrigation, and knowledge. We equip farmers so they move from survival farming to secure harvests and income — for this season and the next.

Agriculture is not a side project. It is the primary pathway we use so communities stop depending on aid and start feeding themselves.

  • Quality, climate-suited seeds and farming inputs
  • Essential tools and equipment
  • Hands-on agricultural training and knowledge transfer
  • Pathways to market and surplus income
Empower a Farmer
03
Food & Relief

Meet today’s hunger. Build tomorrow’s harvest.

When families are in crisis, food assistance is an act of dignity and love. We provide emergency support while always linking it to longer-term solutions — seeds, tools, training, and water — so relief becomes a bridge, not a permanent destination.

  • Emergency food assistance with dignity
  • Relief paired with agricultural and water pathways
  • Support for the most vulnerable households
Feed a Family
04
Faith & Community

Spiritual and practical transformation together.

We partner with local churches and community leaders. Lasting change is owned by the people we serve — not by outsiders. Faith is not an add-on; it is the foundation that calls us to action and sustains hope when circumstances are hard.

  • Partnership with local churches and leaders
  • Dignity, hope, and the love of Christ demonstrated in practice
  • Strengthening community capacity for the long term
Partner With Us
Based & Incorporated in Texas

Serving our neighbors at home

As a Texas-based ministry, we do not only work overseas. We operate food pantries, support housing and schooling, run programs for children, campaign against domestic violence, and walk with single mothers — with the same heart of service.

05
Texas Local Outreach

Food, housing, school & kids programs

Families in our Texas communities face empty pantries, unstable housing, and educational gaps. We meet practical needs close to home — food pantries, housing assistance, school support, and programs that give children stability and hope.

  • Food pantries and emergency food support
  • Housing assistance and stability support
  • Schooling support and educational help
  • Kids programs that nurture and protect
Support Local Families

Freedom for the oppressed

06
Against Domestic Violence

We campaign. We walk with survivors.

Domestic violence is a hidden crisis in our communities. As a faith ministry rooted in Luke 4, we are called to set the oppressed free. We campaign against domestic violence and walk with survivors toward safety, healing, and restored dignity.

  • Community awareness and campaign against domestic violence
  • Practical support for survivors seeking safety
  • Dignity, confidentiality, and Christ-centered care
Stand With Survivors
07
Single Mothers & Children

So no family faces hardship alone.

Single mothers often carry the weight of providing, parenting, and protecting with limited support. We offer practical help, mentoring, food support, and educational programs for mothers and their children — so families can stand stronger.

  • Practical help and mentoring for single mothers
  • Food support and emergency assistance
  • Educational programs for children
Help a Mother & Child
One Mission

How it all connects

Whether a village gains a well, a farmer receives seeds and training, or a single mother finds food and safety in Texas — every act is part of the same calling: good news to the poor, freedom for the oppressed, and hope that lasts.
