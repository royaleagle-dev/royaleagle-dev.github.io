About Harvest of Hope

A Texas-based and incorporated Christian ministry called to proclaim good news to the poor — through clean water, sustainable agriculture, local outreach, and standing with the oppressed.

Who We Are

Faith in action — from Texas to Africa.

Harvest of Hope is a Texas-based and incorporated 501(c)(3) nonprofit Christian ministry. We are headquartered in the Dallas–Fort Worth area and governed under Texas law as a public charity.

Our calling is clear: to bring practical help and lasting hope to underserved communities — overseas and right here at home.

Our Mission

To equip families and communities with clean water, food security, sustainable agriculture, and the practical support they need to move from dependency to dignity — while demonstrating the love of Christ and standing with the vulnerable, including survivors of domestic violence and single mothers.

Where We Serve

Two fronts. One calling.

We work across Africa and right here in Texas — meeting urgent needs while building long-term pathways out of poverty and oppression.

Overseas

Communities Across Africa

Clean water projects, sustainable agriculture, food relief, and faith partnerships with local churches. We equip farmers with seeds, tools, training, and market pathways so harvest becomes lasting security — not just temporary aid.

  • Wells, boreholes & water access
  • Seeds, tools, irrigation & agricultural training
  • Food assistance paired with long-term solutions
  • Partnership with local churches and leaders
Local · Texas

Families in Texas

As a ministry based and incorporated in Texas, we serve our own communities: food pantries, housing and school support, programs for children, advocacy against domestic violence, and practical care for single mothers — so no family faces hardship alone.

  • Food pantries & emergency relief
  • Housing & schooling assistance
  • Kids programs & educational support
  • Campaign against domestic violence
  • Care for single mothers & children
Our Approach

From relief to self-sufficiency

We don’t want families to depend on aid forever. Sustainable agriculture and local partnership are the bridge to lasting change.

1

Meet the need

Emergency food, clean water, and safety for those in crisis — with dignity.

2

Equip & train

Seeds, tools, irrigation, farming knowledge, and practical skills that last.

3

Harvest & income

Families produce food for themselves and surplus that can become income.

4

Stronger community

Local churches and leaders carry the work forward — generational hope.
