Texas — our home base

Harvest of Hope is based and incorporated in Texas. From our Dallas–Fort Worth headquarters we serve families in our own communities while coordinating work overseas.

Local outreach includes food pantries, housing and school support, programs for children, advocacy against domestic violence, and practical care for single mothers.
