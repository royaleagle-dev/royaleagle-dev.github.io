The Lore

Sterling and Dylan first locked eyes at NOCCA — the New Orleans Center for Creative Arts — in the visual art department's roll call room. Full circle, as they now wed in the NOCCA culinary arts building.

They met again when Sterling joined Dylan's first band, Royal T, as their new bass player. Though both were seeing other people, they had an immediate connection writing music together.

After that band dissolved, they went their separate ways for two years until they happened to reconnect. Now both single, the chemistry was obvious and it all fell into place.

It's been eight years of music, art, the adventure of growing up together, and building a life from their love of it all.
