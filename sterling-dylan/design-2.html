Chapter I

The Lore

Sterling and Dylan first locked eyes at NOCCA (New Orleans Center for Creative Arts) in the visual art departments' roll call room — full circle as they now get married in the NOCCA culinary arts building.

They met again when Sterling joined Dylan's first band, Royal T, as their new bass player; though both were seeing other people, they had an immediate connection writing music together.

After that band dissolved, they both went their separate ways for two years until they happened to reconnect. Now both single, the chemistry was obvious and it all fell into place.

It's been eight years of music, art, the adventure of growing up together, and building a life from their love of it all.

Immediately following the ceremony, we'll celebrate with a taco bar, pupusas, and other Nicaraguan delicacies. Toasts will be made, flan will be cut, and the dance floor will await. The reception includes an open bar with signature cocktails inspired by the couple's favorite New Orleans spirits.

Dress Code

Creative Black Tie — We encourage you to express yourself! Think elegant with a touch of artistic flair. The venue has both indoor and outdoor spaces, so consider comfortable footwear for dancing. New Orleans evenings in October are mild and pleasant.

Chapter III

For The Travelers

Everything you need to know for your journey to New Orleans

Accommodations

We have reserved a block of rooms at The Pontchartrain Hotel (2031 St Charles Ave) at a discounted rate. Use code STERDYLAN24 when booking. Alternative options include The Old No. 77 and The Quisby.

Getting There

Fly into Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY). The venue is a 25-minute drive from the airport. Uber/Lyft are readily available. Street parking is limited near Press Street — we recommend rideshare or the hotel shuttle.

Things to Do

Make a weekend of it! Visit the French Quarter, catch live jazz on Frenchmen Street, explore the Bywater neighborhood, or take a steamboat cruise on the Mississippi. The wedding falls during the New Orleans Film Festival — perfect for art lovers.
